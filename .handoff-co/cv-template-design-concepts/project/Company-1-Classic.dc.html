About Us

Meridian Group is an employee-owned advisory and engineering firm helping governments, donors, and private clients deliver resilient infrastructure across East Africa and the Gulf. Since 2009 we have planned, designed, and supervised programmes spanning urban resilience, water, transport, and the built environment — combining technical rigour with deep local partnership. Our multidisciplinary teams work end to end, from feasibility and design through construction supervision and independent evaluation.

Our Mission

To deliver infrastructure that endures — engineered with rigour, built with communities, and managed with integrity.

Our Vision

A region where every city has the resilient, equitable infrastructure its people deserve.

Message from the CEO
DO
Daniel Okafor
Managing Partner & CEO

"We set out to prove that world-class infrastructure could be delivered from within the region, by teams rooted in the communities they serve."

When we founded Meridian Group in 2009, the ambition was simple and stubborn: to plan and build infrastructure to the highest international standard, led by engineers and advisors from the region itself. Fifteen years on, our teams have shaped more than 240 projects across seven countries — roads that connect markets, water systems that reach households, and public institutions built to last.

None of it happens without partnership: with governments who trust us with public mandates, with donors who back ambitious programmes, and above all with the communities who use what we build. Their needs shape every brief we accept and every design we sign.

As we grow, our commitment is unchanged — rigour in every design, integrity in every decision, and a relentless focus on infrastructure that endures. Thank you for being part of our story.

Daniel Okafor
