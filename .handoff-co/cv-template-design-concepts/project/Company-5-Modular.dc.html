About

Meridian Group is an employee-owned advisory and engineering firm helping governments, donors, and private clients deliver resilient infrastructure across East Africa and the Gulf. Since 2009 we have planned, designed, and supervised programmes spanning urban resilience, water, transport, and the built environment — combining technical rigour with deep local partnership.

Mission

Infrastructure that endures — engineered with rigour, built with communities, managed with integrity.

Vision

A region where every city has the resilient, equitable infrastructure its people deserve.

Message from the CEO
DO
Daniel Okafor
Managing Partner & CEO

"We set out to prove that world-class infrastructure could be delivered from within the region, by teams rooted in the communities they serve."

When we founded Meridian Group in 2009, the ambition was simple and stubborn: to plan and build infrastructure to the highest international standard, led by engineers and advisors from the region itself. Fifteen years on, our teams have shaped more than 240 projects across seven countries.

None of it happens without partnership: with governments who trust us with public mandates, with donors who back ambitious programmes, and above all with the communities who use what we build.

As we grow, our commitment is unchanged — rigour in every design, integrity in every decision, and a focus on infrastructure that endures.

Daniel Okafor
